# Critterking
A roguelike where you build the colony you defend. Select and place cards to construct an army and a colony that supports them to beat back the ever-threating invaders!

# Design Goals / Phases
- Jam Build
  - Make engaging and satisfying combat with groups and countering unit types and RTS style combat
  - Day / Night to match Upgrade / Fight, using buildings and unit upgrades to impact battle.
  - Stakes through needing to defend houses
  - Mix the systems of known strategy games (Mini motorways, Bad North, 9 Kings)
- Steam Initial Release
  - Diversify map tiles / beautify world (after harsh graphics ratings as a result of jam)
  - Introduce more decisions / ways to battle through decrees
  - Spice up nights with occasional swarm waves ( after noticing game was felt more fun in later nights, dealing with enemies on multiple sides in large numbers)
  - Introduce risk / reward with charm & expedition system with losing a unit temporarily for an upgrade
  - Space for a greater variety of interactions through more unit types and buildings
  - Incentive to keep playing game after winning once through Adaptations / Challenges / Unlocks
- Update 1 : Critterkingdom
  - Expand game apart from a tactics game and introduce strategic decisions apart from combat (colony mechanics).
  - Expand greatly upon the day time part of the game and tie it closely with the night combat
    - Introduce colony buildings & resource management & daily shop for buildings
    - Militia/Rebels spawning in the night due to colony state
    - Think back to inspiration of Mini Motorways and importance of planning colony with more demand for colony space
  - Balance risk of losing through means of mismanaging colony and slipping up in the nights, both parts should feel important 
# Features
- 13 Unit Types
- 24 Building Types + 2 General Cards
- 13 Unique Charm upgrades + 12 Decree Abilities
- Real Time Tactics Combat, Commanding Units & Active Combat Abilities
- Evolving enemy invaders with varying attack behaviors
- Resource Management & Colony Simulation w/ Trait System
- Adaptation Difficulty Modifiers & Special Challenges
- Save/Load System & Card Unlock System
- Drawing Cards & Building Shop
- Tutorial & Advisor Dialogue
# Design Reflection
- By far the most fun we've had playing our game and more most success developing it. Design started with taking systems from games we liked (combat from bad north, deckbuilding and emphasis on planning from 9 Kings & Mini Motorways), and it helped we were excited to make this game.
- Playtesting for pre-jam build went well, with mostly bugfixes and no major design changes needed. Though we scored lower on graphics than we hoped, the plain map of the jam build was not to player's liking. Procedural generation to bring more life and variety in the map helped with visual aesthetic and allowed us to use terrain as a feature of the game, players now had opportunities to use chokepoints or were prompted with the challenge of natural obstacles.
- For our Steam Release, we planned to add more unit types to have more strategies emerge rather than spamming ranged units. Designing in triangles (rock paper scissors) where each unit would counter each other helped us plan for balance. Though each unit triangle still required playtesting and balancing tweaks to account for interactions with other triangles. Decrees and Charms/Expeditions were added to enhance the combat experience without introducing too much complexity.
- Our major update is built on the basis of evolving the game from a simple RTS style tactics game to a fuller strategy game with colony simulation mechanics and resource management that would tie into the existing combat features. Scope increased dramatically, and we are still finding ways to balance this new system, though the depth and new player decisions it introduces cannot be denied.
